One Accord Support

One Accord helps Apostolic Christian singles find friendship, courtship, and marriage-minded connections with people who share their faith.

Contact us
Questions, feedback, or a safety concern? Email michaelkruzich93@gmail.com. We usually reply within 24 hours.
Report or block someone
Open their profile or your chat with them, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Report or Block. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours.
Delete your account
In the app: Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, likes, matches, and messages.
Premium
Premium is free: invite 3 friends who join with your invite code and it unlocks permanently. There are no subscriptions or purchases.
